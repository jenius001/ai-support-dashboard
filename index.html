About this data and how the numbers are definedClick to expandClick to collapse
  • Simulated data. Every conversation is fictional, generated for a hiring assessment. This is a prototype, not a live system. All thresholds are illustrative, to agree with the client.
  • Filters. The week drives the six numbers, the alerts and the charts. From and To narrow the conversation table to days within that week.
  • QA sample. Each week, a random sample of conversations, stratified by outcome, is reviewed: — this week. QA pass rate and right-call rate both come from this same random sample. Targeted risk audits are reported separately in Quality & Audits, so they never skew the QA pass rate. Unreviewed conversations are shown as Not reviewed, never as passed.
  • QA pass rate vs right-call rate. QA pass rate is pass or soft pass. Right-call rate is whether the agent correctly chose to resolve, clarify or escalate. QA rubric v1 scored the reply and penalized missed escalations, but not unnecessary ones. That's why — wrong calls this week still passed QA. Fixing the rubric is a calibration item.
  • Repeat contacts count a customer coming back about the same issue within 7 days of an AI-handled conversation. The latest week is provisional until 7 days have passed, so its figure can still rise.
  • Customer resolution is separate from QA: a conversation can pass QA but still be pending with a person, or come back as a repeat contact.
  • SLA (illustrative). A person responds within — of a handoff. Waiting past that is overdue, and counts as an SLA breach.
